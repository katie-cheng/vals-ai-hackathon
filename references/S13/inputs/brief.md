# S13 brief (verbatim from `sites[].prompt`)

```text
Completely transform my existing cleaning company's website into an extremely animation-heavy, visually impressive, premium website. I want an unusually high amount of movement and interactive visual elements. Do not create a basic contractor website with a few fade-in animations. I want the entire website to feel alive, immersive, dynamic, and professionally designed. Think of the visual quality of an award-winning interactive website, combined with the polished presentation of a luxury automotive brand. IMPORTANT: Prioritize animations, moving elements, interactive effects, transitions, and visual storytelling. I want motion throughout practically every section of the website.
## 1. AN EXTREMELY IMPRESSIVE HERO SECTION
Make the opening screen unforgettable.
- Full-screen cinematic background featuring professional exterior house cleaning.
- Animated water spraying across the screen.
- Realistic moving water droplets and splashes.
- Animated sunlight reflections on clean windows.
- Subtle moving particles and atmospheric effects.
- Large typography that animates into position.
- Animated headlines with words appearing individually.
- Moving background layers with parallax depth.
- Floating water droplets that respond to cursor movement.
- Animated CTA buttons with sophisticated hover effects.
- A dramatic transition into the next section as the user scrolls.
The hero should immediately communicate professional, high-quality cleaning services.
## 2. WATER AND CLEANING ANIMATIONS
Create custom animations specifically related to the cleaning industry.
Include:
- Realistic water flowing across surfaces.
- Pressure washer spray animations.
- Water droplets rolling down glass.
- Animated cleaning foam and bubbles.
- Glass becoming visibly cleaner.
- Dirt and grime visually disappearing.
- Animated reflections appearing on cleaned windows.
- Water splashing and flowing off surfaces.
- Subtle sparkle effects after cleaning.
- Animated cleaning equipment and hoses.
These effects should look sophisticated and realistic rather than cartoonish.
## 3. ANIMATED BEFORE-AND-AFTER SHOWCASES
Create large interactive before-and-after demonstrations.
Include:
- House siding before and after pressure washing.
- Dirty and clean driveways.
- Dirty and clean exterior windows.
- Dirty and clean interior windows.
- Clogged and cleaned gutters.
- Dirty and restored patios.
Make these interactive with draggable comparison sliders that reveal the cleaning transformation.
Add smooth transitions, animated labels, moving handles, and subtle visual effects.
## 4. EXTREMELY ANIMATED SERVICES SECTION
Every service should have its own unique animated presentation.
**Pressure Washing**
- Animated pressure washer spray.
- Water particles and splash effects.
- Surface cleaning animations.
**Exterior Window Cleaning**
- Animated squeegee movements.
- Water droplets moving down glass.
- Window reflections becoming clearer.
**Interior Window Cleaning**
- Animated glass cleaning.
- Subtle reflections and sunlight.
- Smooth transitions from cloudy glass to crystal-clear glass.
**Gutter Cleaning**
- Animated leaves moving through gutters.
- Visual gutter cleaning demonstrations.
- Water flowing freely through cleaned gutters.
**House Washing**
- Animated dirt disappearing from siding.
- Water spraying across house exteriors.
- Clean surfaces being revealed.
**Driveway and Patio Cleaning**
- Animated surface restoration.
- Water movement across concrete.
- Before-and-after transformations.
Make service cards interactive, with 3D tilting, moving graphics, hover animations, and smooth transitions.
## 5. MASSIVE SCROLL-BASED ANIMATIONS
I want the website to have an incredible amount of scroll-triggered motion.
Implement:
- Sections that animate into view.
- Images that expand as users scroll.
- Text that reveals itself progressively.
- Backgrounds that change smoothly.
- Elements that move at different scroll speeds.
- Sticky sections with changing content.
- Horizontal scrolling service showcases.
- Animated number counters.
- Moving graphic elements that connect sections.
- Smooth zoom-in and zoom-out effects.
- Layered parallax scrolling.
- Scroll-controlled cleaning demonstrations.
- Sections that transition into each other instead of simply stacking.
Use advanced animation techniques to make scrolling feel like an interactive experience.
## 6. INTERACTIVE BACKGROUNDS
Avoid static backgrounds wherever possible.
Use:
- Slowly moving water textures.
- Animated gradients.
- Floating bubbles.
- Moving light reflections.
- Subtle particles.
- Animated water ripples.
- Flowing lines and shapes.
- Moving background photography.
- Cursor-responsive visual elements.
- Layered depth and perspective effects.
Keep everything coordinated with the cleaning company's branding.
## 7. INTERACTIVE NAVIGATION AND BUTTONS
Make the entire interface feel responsive.
- Animated navigation bar.
- Smooth dropdown menus.
- Animated mobile navigation.
- Buttons with magnetic hover effects.
- Animated arrows.
- Interactive icons.
- Moving underlines.
- Smooth page transitions.
- Cursor-responsive buttons.
- Animated quote and booking buttons.
## 8. ANIMATED PROCESS SECTION
Create an impressive visual explanation of how the cleaning service works.
Show:
1. Customer requests a free estimate.
2. Cleaning service is scheduled.
3. Professional cleaning is performed.
4. Customer receives a beautifully cleaned property.
Animate each stage with its own custom graphics, transitions, and movement.
Connect each step with animated lines or flowing water effects.
## 9. TRUST AND PROFESSIONALISM
Create an attractive section highlighting:
- Professional service.
- Attention to detail.
- Reliable scheduling.
- Quality results.
- Residential cleaning services.
- Interior and exterior window cleaning.
Use animated icons, interactive cards, and subtle motion.
Do not invent certifications, insurance coverage, testimonials, ratings, or years of experience.
## 10. CONTACT SECTION
Make the quote request section visually impressive.
- Animated background.
- Moving typography.
- Interactive form fields.
- Smooth focus animations.
- Animated buttons.
- Attractive success and error messages.
- Responsive mobile layout.
Preserve all existing contact functionality. All quote requests and contact form submissions must continue going to the company's configured email address.
## 11. ANIMATED FOOTER
Create a dramatic footer with:
- Large animated typography.
- Moving background elements.
- Animated navigation links.
- Subtle water effects.
- Interactive social icons.
- A prominent animated quote button.
## 12. TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION
Use advanced web animation techniques.
Consider:
- GSAP.
- GSAP ScrollTrigger.
- Framer Motion.
- CSS keyframe animations.
- SVG animations.
- Canvas-based particle effects.
- WebGL where genuinely beneficial.
- Interactive mouse tracking.
- Smooth scrolling.
- 3D transforms and perspective.
Do not use every technology unnecessarily. Choose the best combination for smooth performance.
Optimize for desktop, tablet, and mobile. Ensure animations do not cause lag, broken layouts, or excessive loading times. Respect reduced-motion accessibility settings.
## 13. FINAL DESIGN DIRECTION
The finished website should feel:
- Extremely dynamic.
- Premium and professional.
- Visually immersive.
- Modern and sophisticated.
- Realistic rather than cartoonish.
- Rich in movement and interactive details.
Use high-quality real-world cleaning photography and realistic visual effects.
Avoid generic contractor templates, static service cards, boring backgrounds, and repetitive fade-in animations.
FINAL REQUIREMENT:
I want substantially more animation than a typical business website. Almost every section should contain multiple moving or interactive elements.
Be ambitious. Add layers of movement, sophisticated transitions, interactive cleaning demonstrations, and visually impressive details.
Do not sacrifice usability for animation. Visitors must still be able to quickly understand the services, request a quote, and contact the business.
Actually implement the complete redesign, not just a visual mockup. Preserve all existing business information and working functionality. Test the entire website on desktop and mobile before finishing.
```
