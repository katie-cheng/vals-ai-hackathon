## What the golden does
- Deterministic 7 x 8 board logic in separate modules under `src/game/logic/` (placement, orthogonal match detection, clearing, cascades, frozen pebbles, flowers, stones, locks, scoring and stars, objectives, moves, boosters, outcome, reshuffle recovery). The state machine (`READY > DROPPING > MATCHING > CLEARING > CASCADING > READY`, plus `LEVEL_COMPLETE`, `LEVEL_FAILED`, `PAUSED`) lives in `src/game/controller.ts` and blocks input outside `READY`.
- 40 levels in `src/game/levels/chapter1-4.ts` (Mossy Grove, Frost Hollow, Blossom Creek, Autumn Ridge). `npm run verify-levels` plays each one with a lookahead bot and confirms it can be won within its move limit.
- A Phaser 3 `JarScene` draws the glass jar, rope, wooden base, falling pebbles, pulse, particles and score popups. React draws every screen and the HUD, plus an accessible DOM grid over the jar.
- Original art made from SVG (`tools/art/`) rendered to PNG sprites and WebP backgrounds. Synthesized WAV sounds and woodland music. Music, sound, vibration and language (en/es/fr) are separate settings.
- No pills, eyebrows, emoji icons, gradient text or em dashes. Controls fit from 360px to desktop without wrapping or overlapping.

## Invented inputs
None. The prompt names no external assets or repos. Every artwork and sound is generated by the golden's own scripts.

## Task checks (`tests/task.mjs`)
These work through visible labels and the move counter, and accept a canvas or a DOM grid board. The checks cover: main menu items, Phaser canvas, level intro, HUD, a drop that uses a move, double-input protection, the three boosters with counts and cancel, pause with exit confirmation, 40 numbered levels in 4 chapters with locks, settings that persist and toggle independently, help/about pages, shop purchase persisting in localStorage with no real money, daily challenge, achievements, WebP and PNG art, audio after interaction, no permission requests, and phone controls that do not overlap.

## Shared verifier issues
- The gate (`text >= 200` and `height >= 600` on `/`) scores both original game builds 0 before any task check runs. A game whose first screen is a splash or a short menu fails it no matter how complete the game is. Task-level checks cannot affect this.
