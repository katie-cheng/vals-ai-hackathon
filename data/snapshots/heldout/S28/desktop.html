Welcome

Amazigh hospitality, as it has always been lived

Mohamed welcomes you together with his wife and their two sons in their traditional home in Ghassate, near Ouarzazate. Here, time slows down. You share bread, mint tea and stories by the fire, surrounded by mountains and a garden of almond trees.

This is not a hotel: it is a home that opens its doors to you. You will leave feeling you were received as one of the family.

The stay

Everything that makes a stay here so gentle

Three bedrooms, a large traditional Moroccan living room, hammam, fireplace and a terrace open to the mountains.

The family table

Homemade breakfast, bread baked over the fire, garden jams and tagines prepared with heart.

Truly authentic experiences

Traditional hammam, garden and farm discovery, village walks and a visit to the old Kasbah.
