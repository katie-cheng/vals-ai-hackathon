The house

A door left open onto the valley.

In Ghassate, a rural commune near Ouarzazate, Mohamed's home opens onto a garden, a terrace and mountains that change colour with the light of the day.

There is no reception desk or hotel corridor here: you share the large traditional living room, taste the morning bread, and let yourself be guided to the hammam, the garden and the old village.

Our story

Hospitality in four gestures

Sleep, taste, unwind, wander.

  • 01SleepThree bedrooms, a large traditional Moroccan living room, a private bathroom and the calm of a family home.
  • 02TasteHomemade breakfast, bread, garden jams and traditional tagines on request.
  • 03UnwindTraditional hammam, fireplace, billiards and a terrace facing the mountains.
  • 04WanderGarden and farm, village walks, the old kasbah and the community museum.
