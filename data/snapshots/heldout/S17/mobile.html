How do I book an appointment?

Use “Book an appointment” to share your name, preferred service and date, then send your request through WhatsApp. You can also call +91 70840 07700. Your appointment is confirmed only after our team replies and agrees on a time.

Is SHE exclusively for women?

Yes. SHE SALON & MAKE-UP STUDIO is a women-only beauty destination, with makeup, hairstyling and beauty services in one place.

Where can I check prices and opening hours?

Please call or message us on WhatsApp for current service prices, availability and the complete weekly opening schedule. Confirm your preferred time before visiting the salon.

Can I discuss my bridal look before booking?

Absolutely. Share your wedding date, outfit, jewellery and any makeup or hair inspiration on WhatsApp. The team can discuss your ideas, available services and appointment options with you.
